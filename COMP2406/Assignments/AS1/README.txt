ADDITIONAL DESIGN DECISIONS
------------------------------
- A few small accessibility touches beyond what was asked: aria-label on
  the shelter dropdown, aria-pressed on the pet-type filter buttons,
  aria-current="page" on the active nav link, and a visible focus outline
  for keyboard navigation.
